Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Jarvis Backup is a personal tool operated by Nir Sher for his own use only.

What data the app accesses

The app uses Google Drive access solely to upload, list and restore backup files created by the owner, in the owner's own Google Drive account.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only to create and maintain the owner's backups. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any models. No data from any other Google user is accessed.

Storage and retention

Backup files stay in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
